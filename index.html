📖 How to play

😇 Civilians all get the same secret word. 🕵️ Undercover gets a very similar word. 🎭 Mr. White gets no word at all and must bluff.

Pass the phone around so everyone sees their word in secret. Then, starting from the player shown, each person gives a one-word clue about their word. Discuss, vote, and tap the player you want out.

Winning: Civilians win when every impostor is out. Impostors win when they outnumber the civilians or only one civilian is left. If Mr. White is voted out, they get one guess at the civilians' word to steal the round. Word forms count (care, cares, caring) and one small typo in a long word is forgiven.

Smart words: every pair is a near-twin with one sharp difference. A word used in a round sits out for the next 20 rounds. In Mixed mode the game goes harder after three quick civilian wins and easier after two impostor wins. All Undercovers share one word unless you switch on Split Undercover words, which gives each of them a different nearby word so they cannot spot each other.

Points: Civilians win = 2 each (1 if you were voted out). Undercover win = 5. Mr. White guesses right = 10.
